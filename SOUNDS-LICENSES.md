# Sound sources and licenses

Every sound in `packs/` is a genuine recording from the sources below. Licenses were checked on each
source page on 2026-11-03 (Asia/Manila). Freesound files were taken from the public HQ preview
(same recording, same license as the original upload). All files were trimmed, split into
press/release, resampled to 48 kHz mono and level-matched by `tools/build_packs.py`. Some special-key
samples (noted below) are pitch-shifted copies of the same recording. No sounds from Keeby or any
commercial product are used.

## Buckling Spring (`packs/buckling`)
- Source: *bucklespring* by Ico Doornekamp, https://github.com/zevv/bucklespring (commit 43274ed), `wav/` directory:
  his own per-key recordings of an IBM Model M (press and release for each key).
- License: **GNU GPL v2** (repository license, copy in `packs/buckling/LICENSE-GPL-2.0.txt`).
  Fine for personal use and modification; if you ever redistribute this pack, do so under GPL-2.0 with that notice.

## Clicky Blue (`packs/clicky-blue`)
- "Single Mechanical Keyboard Tap.wav" by Professor-Syntax, https://freesound.org/people/Professor-Syntax/sounds/523768/ — **CC0 1.0**
  (normal keys; enter, backspace and modifiers are a 0.9x pitch copy).
- "spacebarKey.wav" by UberBosser, https://freesound.org/people/UberBosser/sounds/421582/ — **CC0 1.0** (spacebar).

## Tactile Brown (`packs/tactile-brown`)
- "Keyboard - Press down" by Foxfire-, https://freesound.org/people/Foxfire-/sounds/570754/ — **CC0 1.0** (press; two variants are ±4 % pitch copies).
- "Keyboard - Key release" by Foxfire-, https://freesound.org/people/Foxfire-/sounds/570755/ — **CC0 1.0** (release).
- "Single_Key_Stroke_Large_Key" by Capt.Jack, https://freesound.org/people/Capt.Jack/sounds/813210/ — **CC0 1.0** (space, enter, backspace).

## Soft Tactile (`packs/soft-tactile`)
- "Single Key Presses on Mechanical Keyboard" by Xemptful, https://freesound.org/people/Xemptful/sounds/660387/ — **CC0 1.0**
  (5 press/release pairs; space/enter/backspace are slightly lower-pitched copies).

## Deep Clear (`packs/deep-clear`)
- "Mechanical keyboard clicking. Different keys (4)" by humi74, https://freesound.org/people/humi74/sounds/412926/ — **CC0 1.0**
  (Cherry MX Clear; 8 press/release pairs, light noise reduction; space is a lower-pitched copy).

## Mouse clicks (`packs/_mouse`, shared by all packs)
- "computer mouse click" by florianreichelt, https://freesound.org/people/florianreichelt/sounds/683099/ — **CC0 1.0**
  (right and middle buttons are slight pitch copies).
